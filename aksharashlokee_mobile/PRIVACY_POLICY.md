# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 28, 2026

**Aksharashlokee** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Aksharashlokee").

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the Application:
- **Personal Data:** Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and account credentials provided when registering for an account.
- **Usage Data:** Information about your interaction with the app, such as shlokas viewed, bookmarks, and learning progress.
- **Device Data:** Basic device information (such as operating system version) to ensure compatibility and improve app performance.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your user account.
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Aksharashlokee application.
- Facilitate user role capabilities (Learner, Editor, Admin).
- Send account verification and authentication notifications (e.g., OTP emails).
- Improve app functionality and user experience.

---

### 3. Data Sharing and Security
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We implement reasonable administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion
We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may request account deletion or data removal by contacting us.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Aksharashlokee does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
